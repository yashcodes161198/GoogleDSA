# Google DSA Prep

A personal practice space for a fixed library of interview questions, with per-user progress and a daily revision queue.

## Language

**Library**:
The shared set of interview questions you can browse, filter, and practice.
_Avoid_: Catalog, question bank, problem list

**Progress**:
Your state on one library question: whether you have solved it, how many times you have revised it, whether it is a favorite, and your solve times.
_Avoid_: Status row, user problem

**Revision**:
A revisit of a question you have already solved, after the cooldown. It is counted apart from the first time you solve it.
_Avoid_: Review

**Review**:
The spaced-repetition due date stored for a solved question. It is not the daily revision queue.
_Avoid_: Revision

**Revision queue**:
Today's solved questions that are ready to revise, capped by the daily limit, ordered from least revised.
_Avoid_: Review list, due list
